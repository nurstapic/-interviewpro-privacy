InterviewPro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

InterviewPro (“InterviewPro,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the InterviewPro application.

1. Information We Collect

InterviewPro may receive information that you voluntarily provide when using the app, including:

* Interview videos that you choose to upload for evaluation.
* Information you voluntarily provide in connection with your interview or evaluation.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily include it in information or content you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

Interview videos and other information you submit are used solely to provide the interview evaluation service.

Interview videos may be reviewed manually by InterviewPro for the purpose of evaluating interview performance against InterviewPro’s predefined evaluation criteria.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your interview videos for advertising.

3. Storage and Sharing

InterviewPro does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We do not share interview videos with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where information is temporarily processed or stored as necessary to provide the service, it is handled only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain submitted content only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the evaluation service and fulfill the purpose for which it was submitted, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

5. Children’s Privacy

InterviewPro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through InterviewPro. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.
